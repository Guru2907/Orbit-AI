# Refund Policy

Flowspace offers refunds under the following conditions:

- If you are charged within the first 14 days of a paid subscription and
  are not satisfied, you can request a full refund.
- Refund requests must be submitted through the billing page or by
  contacting support at support@flowspace.example.com.
- Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days back to the original
  payment method.
- After the first 14 days of a billing cycle, charges for that cycle are
  non-refundable, but you can cancel to prevent future charges.
- Annual plans: if canceled within the first 30 days of an annual
  subscription, a prorated refund is issued for the unused months.
- Refunds are not provided for partial months of usage after the initial
  refund window has passed.

Flowspace does not offer refunds for accounts suspended due to violations
of the Terms of Service.
