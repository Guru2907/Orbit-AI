# How to Cancel Your Subscription

You can cancel your Flowspace subscription at any time. Cancelling stops
future billing but does not delete your data immediately.

Steps to cancel:
1. Log in to your Flowspace account.
2. Go to Settings > Billing.
3. Click "Cancel Subscription."
4. Confirm cancellation when prompted.

After cancelling:
- You retain access to paid features until the end of your current
  billing period.
- Your account then automatically moves to the Free plan.
- If your team size or usage exceeds Free plan limits, some boards or
  members may become read-only until you either upgrade again or reduce
  usage to fit Free plan limits.
- Your data is retained for 90 days after downgrade in case you want to
  resubscribe. After 90 days, data may be permanently deleted.

To cancel an annual plan, the same steps apply; see the Refund Policy
document for information about prorated refunds within 30 days.
