# Flowspace Pricing

Flowspace offers three plans, billed monthly or annually. Annual billing
gives a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.

## Free Plan
- Price: $0
- Up to 3 team members
- Up to 2 active boards
- 1 GB file storage
- Community support only

## Pro Plan
- Price: $8 per user per month (billed annually), or $10 per user per month
  (billed monthly)
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited boards
- 50 GB file storage
- Priority email support
- Advanced reporting

## Business Plan
- Price: $16 per user per month (billed annually), or $20 per user per
  month (billed monthly)
- Everything in Pro
- Single sign-on (SSO)
- Admin controls and audit logs
- Dedicated onboarding support
- 500 GB file storage

All plans include a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan features. No credit
card is required to start the trial.
