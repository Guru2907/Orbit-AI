# Inviting Team Members

Flowspace makes it easy to add teammates to your workspace.

Steps to invite a teammate:
1. Go to Settings > Team Members.
2. Click "Invite Member."
3. Enter their email address.
4. Choose their role: Admin, Member, or Viewer.
5. Click "Send Invite."

The invited person receives an email with a link to join. Invite links
expire after 7 days if not accepted.

Roles explained:
- Admin: full access, can manage billing, invite/remove members, and
  change workspace settings.
- Member: can create and edit boards and tasks, cannot access billing or
  remove other members.
- Viewer: read-only access to boards, cannot edit tasks.

On the Free plan, workspaces are limited to 3 team members total. Upgrade
to Pro or Business for unlimited team members.
