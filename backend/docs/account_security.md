# Account Security

Flowspace takes account security seriously.

Password requirements:
- Minimum 10 characters
- Must include at least one number and one symbol
- Cannot reuse your last 5 passwords

Two-factor authentication (2FA):
- Available on all plans.
- Can be enabled under Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication.
- Supports authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) via QR code.

Single sign-on (SSO):
- Available on the Business plan only.
- Supports SAML-based SSO providers including Okta, Azure AD, and Google
  Workspace.

If you suspect unauthorized access to your account:
1. Change your password immediately under Settings > Security.
2. Enable 2FA if not already active.
3. Review active sessions under Settings > Security > Active Sessions and
   sign out any unfamiliar devices.
4. Contact support at support@flowspace.example.com if you need further
   help securing your account.

Flowspace encrypts data in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256).
